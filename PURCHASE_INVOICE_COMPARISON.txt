CALCULATION FORMULAS:
════════════════════

Item Level:
───────────
Disc Amt = (Pur Price × Qty) × (Disc % / 100)
Before Tax = (Pur Price × Qty) - Disc Amt
AdvAmount = Before Tax × (AdvTax% / 100)
Line Total = Before Tax + AdvAmount
Margin % = ((Sale Price - Pur Price) / Pur Price) × 100

Invoice Level:
──────────────
Subtotal = Sum of all Line Totals
Invoice Disc = Subtotal × (Inv Disc % / 100) OR Flat Amount
After Disc = Subtotal - Invoice Disc
Invoice AdvTax = After Disc × (Inv AdvTax % / 100)
Net Payable = After Disc + Invoice AdvTax
Balance = Net Payable - Payment
