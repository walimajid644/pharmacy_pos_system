HOW TO ADD YOUR LOGO:
═════════════════════

1. Go to: Settings → Receipt tab

2. Find: "Pharmacy Logo (Optional)" 
   (It's at the TOP of the form)

3. Enter your logo file path:
   Examples:
   • logo.png
   • ./assets/logo.png
   • C:/Pharmacy/logo.png

4. Check the preview:
   - Scroll to the TOP of the preview
   - You'll see: [LOGO] box at the very top
   - Below it: your file path


LOGO APPEARS:
════════════

✓ In Settings preview (as [LOGO] placeholder)
✓ On printed receipts (currently as placeholder)
✓ ALWAYS at the very top, above company name
✓ Only if you enter a logo path (optional)
